# Code of conduct

We commit to a respectful, inclusive, evidence-led community. Critique claims and systems, not people. Accept correction, disclose uncertainty, protect private information, and never use provenance to shame a contributor or agent.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, disclosure of private information, deliberate misrepresentation of evidence, and public disclosure of an unpatched vulnerability are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact on the [smilinTux organization profile](https://github.com/smilinTux). Maintainers will protect reporter privacy and respond proportionately.

This policy applies to repository activity and official project spaces.
